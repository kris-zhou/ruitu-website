Ruitu (Hong Kong) Trading Co., Ltd.

As a leading beauty products supplier, we are dedicated to providing excellent cross-border beauty wholesale services to global customers.

Our company has direct access to Korean duty-free beauty product supply chains, covering numerous international renowned beauty brands such as NARS, Tom Ford, La Prairie, Estée Lauder, Fresh, and more, ensuring our product line always stays at the forefront of fashion and quality.

RuiTu & COSMETICS is the ITALIAN LUXURY beauty division of the B2B wholesale and trading company, specializing in providing a wide range of products in the fashion and luxury sector.

AP ITALIAN LUXURY was founded in 2011 by Alessandra Perrani and Stefano Brasini. The company has expanded to numerous countries worldwide, serving online and wholesale business customer departments.

AP BEAUTY & COSMETICS brand was established in 2016, aimed at providing quality branded cosmetics, pharmacy cosmetics, perfumes, and home fragrances primarily to B2B customers in Asia.

The company begins with an in-depth analysis of each customer's original requirements through to order collection at its logistics warehouse. Then 100% of the products undergo quality checks with internal staff support.

Each shipment is carefully packaged and protected to prevent damage during transport. Photos and videos of each item are taken to identify any defects and provided to the customer along with accurate quality reports.
